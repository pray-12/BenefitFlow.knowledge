BenefitFlow provides powerful filtering capabilities to help you target and manage your prospects efficiently across different tabs.

Key Features:

- Comprehensive Filtering: Access advanced filters on Broker, Broker Contacts, Employers, and Employer Contacts tabs.
- Customizable Ideal Customer Profile: Build precise filters to match your go-to-market strategy, including:
	- Geographic location
	- Company size
	- Insurance type (e.g., self-funded groups)
	- Specific insurance offerings
	- Much more!
- Exclude Filters: Use exclusion options to identify unique market opportunities, such as brokers not offering specific insurance types.
- Saved Searches:
	- Save your custom filter combinations
	- Quickly access your preferred searches on each login
	- Maintain consistent prospecting approach
- List Management:
	- Select and create lists of broker offices, broker contacts, employers, or employer contacts
	- Export lists to CSV
	- Push lists directly to your CRM
	- Track and manage your target accounts
- Seamless Workflow: Filters persist when navigating between different sections, allowing for smooth exploration of broker and employer profiles.

Maximize your prospecting efficiency by leveraging BenefitFlow's advanced filtering and list management tools.
