## ✅ What New Users Should Know

Here are the top things to keep in mind when logging in for the first time:

### 1\. You’ll See Three Primary Tabs

- **By Broker** – Reverse-engineered client lists of every benefits broker, by office.
- **By Employer** – Search U.S. employers with benefits data, renewal dates, and sentiment insights.
- **By Contact** – Access 100K+ verified broker and decision-maker contacts.

### 2\. Every Filter Updates the Platform in Real Time

As you adjust filters (location, funding type, renewal date, etc.), the system instantly updates brokers, employers, and contacts that match.

### 3\. Your Credits Only Count When You Click “Get Contact”

You can view firmographics and filters freely—credits are only used when revealing verified emails and phone number, adding to a list, or exporting contacts.

### 4\. Most Data Is Exportable

Click the three-dot menu on any result to export broker lists, employer profiles, or contact records to CSV—or sync directly to your CRM (if integrated).

### 5\. You Can Always Press “More Info”

On any broker or employer result, click **More Info** to dive deeper into:

- Client lists
- Broker office locations
- Renewal dates
- Carrier relationships
- Sentiment data
- Decision-maker contacts

## 🧭 Platform Navigation Guide

Here’s a quick tour of each tab:

### 🔹 **By Broker Tab** (Start Here)

- See brokerages sorted by highest amount of commissions reported on the 5500
- Click into any firm to see office locations and associated employers (book of business)
- Use filters like geography, employer size, funding type, and renewal date to zero in

**Key Actions:**

- Press **“See Clients”** to view which employers each broker office serves
- Click **“See Contacts”** to access producers/account managers by office

### 🔹 **By Employer Tab**

- Search employers directly by name, location, industry, or headcount
- View employer profiles with:
	- Benefit eligible employee counts
	- Renewal dates
	- Self-funded or fully insured status
	- Sentiment trends and vendor usage

**Key Actions:**

- Use this tab when targeting specific employer accounts
- Filter by employer traits or benefit gaps to create smart outreach lists

### 🔹 **By Contact Tab**

- Search across our full broker + employer contact database
- Filter by job title, location, carrier appointment, tenure, and more
- Great for building outreach or email campaigns

**Key Actions:**

- Use **"Get Contact"** to reveal verified email + phone
- Add contacts to lists and push directly to your CRM
