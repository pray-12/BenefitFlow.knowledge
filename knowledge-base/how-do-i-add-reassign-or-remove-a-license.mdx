BenefitFlow now has a team management page! 

<Note>
**Note**
you must have an "admin" license to complete the below
</Note>

  
If you are an admin logged in to BenefitFlow, you can click your name in the upper right hand corner and click "Team Management"   
  
**BenefitFlow User Admins Have the Ability to:**

- Assign/reassign licenses
- Remove users
- View contact credit utilization by user
- Assign credit limits to users
- Manage and setup a Salesforce Integration

**Please reach out to Customerteam@benefit-flow.com if you need to be an admin for your subscription with us.**
