## Est. Setup Time: 5 minutes

<Warning>
**To setup the integration, you must be a BenefitFlow Admin and a HubSpot Admin with read/write privileges for all fields**
</Warning>

<Steps>
<Step>
**Login to BenefitFlow and** **navigate to the “Integrations” page**

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-26.png" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
**Click the “Establish Connection” button to connect to HubSpot**

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-27.png" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
**Click the “Connect” button to connect to HubSpot**

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-28.png" />
</Frame>
<Tip>
You can connect to either a sandbox or your main production account. 
</Tip>
</Step>
<Step>
**Success!**

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-29.png" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
**To configure your mapping settings, click "Integration Settings"**

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-30.png" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
**Specify how fields across BenefitFlow's employer and broker objects are mapped to your HubSpot fields** 

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-31.png" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
**Once you're ready to begin syncing data, navigate to your lists, click the sync button on the desired list and begin syncing!**

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-32.png" />
</Frame>
<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-33.png" />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>
