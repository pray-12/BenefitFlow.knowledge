## AI-Powered Insights That Turn Hours of Research Into Minutes

### What Is Deep Research?

**Deep Research** is BenefitFlow’s AI-powered research agent that turns scattered public data into a clean, source-linked brief on an employer in seconds. By automatically scanning benefits guides, regulatory filings, careers pages, employee-review sites, press releases, and more, it equips benefits professionals with the context they need—before the first outreach, discovery call, or renewal conversation.

### How It Works

| Layer | What Happens | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1\. Data Layer** | Our crawlers continuously collect unstructured content (PDFs, HTML, forum posts, etc.) and store the raw documents. | Guarantees a wide, up-to-date data footprint. |
| **2\. Intelligence Layer** | Large-language-models extract vendor names, renewal dates, plan design details, employee sentiment, recent changes, and more. | Filters noise and surfaces only the facts that change conversations. |
| **3\. Experience Layer** | BenefitFlow presents insights in a clean, structured report with citations linked back to the original sources. | Saves hours of manual research and makes every data point auditable. |

### Key Use Cases

| Audience | How Deep Research Helps |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Benefits Vendors** | Detect employers with problems your solution fixes (e.g., negative sentiment around current wellness program) and personalize your outreach with confidence. |
| **Carriers** | Spot recent carrier changes, understand how employees feel about the switch, identify coverage gaps, and sense competitive inroads—before the RFP drops. |
| **Brokers** | Walk into discovery calls already knowing the vendor lineup, renewal dates, and hot-button employee issues, so you advise instead of interrogate. |
