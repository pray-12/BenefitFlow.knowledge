### Retirement Intelligence Module

A complete view of employer retirement offerings. Gain instant insight into how each employer’s retirement plans are structured and performing, with standardized Form 5500 data integrated directly into the employer profile.

Highlights:

- Full view of plan design, assets, participation, and provider relationships
- Clear compliance indicators and year-over-year trends
- Search filters for plan type, features, providers, assets, and flags
- Connected with Financial Security insights in Deep Research for a 360° perspective

### P&C Intelligence Module

See benefits and P&C together in one workflow.

The new P&C module brings workers’ comp, OSHA, and DOT data into BenefitFlow — making it easy to understand an employer’s risk profile alongside their benefits.  
  
Highlights:

- Workers’ comp carrier, broker, renewal timing, and X-Mod
- OSHA and DOT inspections, violations, penalties, and long-term trends
- Unified view of benefits + P&C inside every employer profile
- New filters for WC renewals, carriers, X-Mod, OSHA activity, and DOT history
