## Here are some tips and tricks if your temporary password is not working

1. Ensure everything is correct- the temporary password may contain a "." or a ";"
2. Remove extra spaces if copying and pasting email/password

	a. If copying and pasting your email and/or your temporary password into the prompt please ensure you are removing any extra spaces! This will cause an error when logging in
