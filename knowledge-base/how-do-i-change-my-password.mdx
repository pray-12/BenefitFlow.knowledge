---
title: "How do I change my password?"
icon: "circle-question"
---

Please follow the steps below to change your password:

<Steps>
<Step>
Ensure you are logged out of BenefitFlow

    - to log out click your name in the upper right-hand corner and click 'logout'

    - this should bring you to the BenefitFlow main page

</Step>
<Step>
Click 'Login'

</Step>
<Step>
Click 'Forgot your password?"

</Step>
<Step>
Enter your username (your email address)

</Step>
<Step>
Click the reset password
</Step>
</Steps>

**Still having issues?**  
  
  
**Reach out to your admin for your subscription.**  

They will be able to reset your password from the team management page.  

**Don't know who your admin is?**  

Reach out to [customerteam@benefit-flow.com](mailto:customerteam@benefit-flow.com)
