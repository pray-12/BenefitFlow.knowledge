BenefitFlow provides flexible contact credit and data export options to help you access and utilize valuable prospect information.

Key Features:

- Contact Credits:
	- Reveal contact details by using contact credits
	- Credits are only charged for verified email addresses
	- No credits used for phone-only contacts
	- Revealed contacts are shared across your team
	- Automatically saved in the 'All Contacts' list
- Contact Discovery:
	- Click "Get Contact" to view hidden contact details
	- Real-time credit balance notifications
	- Team-wide contact sharing prevents duplicate credit usage
- Export Options:
	- Export broker information with standard or detailed views
	- Export employer data by company or specific Form 5500 insurance policies
	- Access data glossary to understand export field definitions
	- Bulk export capabilities vary by BenefitFlow package
- Additional Info:
	- Broker and employer information exports do not use contact credits
	- Bulk export limits depend on your subscription level
	- Contact customer success team for expanded export capabilities

Bulk Export Limits: The number of records that can be extracted with a single export

- Premium Package
	- Brokerage Firms: 100
	- Brokerage Offices: 300
	- Employers: 5,000
- Ultimate Package
	- Brokerage Firms: 500
	- Brokerage Offices: 2,000
	- Employers: 10,000
- Enterprise Package 
	- Brokerage Firms: 1,000
	- Brokerage Offices: 5,000
	- Employers: 30,000
