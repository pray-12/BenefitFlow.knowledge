## ➕ How to Add Users

<Steps>
<Step>
Log into your BenefitFlow admin account.
</Step>
<Step>
Navigate to your name in the upper right corner **\> Team Management**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click “**Add User**” and enter the new user’s:
	- Full name
	- Work email
	- Role (User or Admin)
</Step>
<Step>
Click “**Send Invite**” – they’ll receive an email to activate their account.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Note:**

Each user added will count toward your license tier.
</Note>

## ❌ How to Delete Users

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Settings > Team Management**.
</Step>
<Step>
Find the user you want to remove.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the “•••” menu next to their name.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **“Remove from Account.”**
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout icon="siren-on" color="#FFC107">
This action immediately revokes access and cannot be undone.
</Callout>


### 📊 View Contact Credit Usage

<Steps>
<Step>
Navigate to Team Management Page **\> Contact**.
</Step>
<Step>
View your monthly contact credit consumption.
</Step>
<Step>
	- Filter by user or date range
	- Export reports if needed
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout icon="thumbtack">
**Reminder:**

Each time a user clicks "Get Contact," it uses 1 credit.
</Callout>

### 🔁 Resetting Passwords

If a user forgets their password:

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to BenefitFlow login page.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **“Forgot Password?”**
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the registered email address.
</Step>
<Step>
Follow the reset instructions sent to your inbox.
</Step>
</Steps>
Admins cannot reset passwords directly on behalf of users.
