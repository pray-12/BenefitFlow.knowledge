### Generating a List of Broker Contacts

**Steps to Build a List:**

<Steps>
<Step title="Accessing the Contacts Tab:">
	- Search by broker and navigate to the contacts tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Using Filters:">
	- Utilize the filters on the left-hand side to create a targeted list of broker contacts.
	- Example: Find Lockton broker contacts in California.
	- Filter by roles or job titles (e.g., producers).
	- Additional filters include years at the company, license type, and carrier appointments.
</Step>
<Step title="Selecting Contacts:">
	- Grab the entire list of broker contacts and click "Get Selected" to use contact credits to obtain email addresses for any contacts you haven't already used a contact credit for.
</Step>
<Step title="Exporting the List:">
	- Export the list into a CSV file or sync to Salesforce.
	- Alternatively, add the contacts to an existing saved list.
</Step>
</Steps>
