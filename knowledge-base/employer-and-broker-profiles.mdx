BenefitFlow provides comprehensive, multi-dimensional profiles for both employers and brokers, offering deep insights into benefits and business relationships.

### Employer Profile Features

- Company Overview:
	- Headquarters location
	- Employee count
	- Funding status (self-funded or fully insured)
	- Primary broker information
- Benefits Tab:
	- Key Partners section (brokers, carriers, vendors)
	- Policy-level insights from Form 5500 filings
	- Filterable policy data (Medical, Dental, Vision)
	- Broker commission and carrier premium summaries
- Detailed Policy Information:
	- Line-item details for each policy
	- Coverage specifics
	- Renewal dates
	- Commissions and premiums
	- Implied Per Employee Per Month (PEPM) costs
- History Tab:
	- Enrollment trends
	- Broker of record changes
	- Carrier modifications
	- Filterable by policy type
- Deep Research Tab:
	- AI-powered insights beyond Form 5500
	- Employee benefit ratings
	- Detailed analysis of:
		- Health Insurance
		- Health and Wellness
		- Family Benefits
		- Career Growth
		- ...and much more
- - Vendor information
	- Employee sentiment analysis
- Contacts Tab:
	- 5500 form signatory
	- Key HR and executive contacts

### Broker Profile Features

- Office Locations:
	- Comprehensive office breakdown
	- Exportable full client list
	- Proprietary client-to-office mapping
- Client Insights:
	- Office-specific client lists
	- Accurate business relationship tracking
- Contact Management:
	- Producer and account manager contacts
	- Verified Producer tags
	- Licensing verification
	- Book of business insights
