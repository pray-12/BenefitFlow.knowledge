## Form 5500 and Contact Data

**Form 5500:** An annual report that every employer in the U.S. is required to file with the Department of Labor. This filing includes several valuable data points, including:

1. The various benefits offered by employers
2. The benefits brokers that employers partner with
3. The insurance carriers that employers pay premiums to

**Proprietary Contact Database:** We have a proprietary 3-step process to generate the contact info in BenefitFlow:

<Steps>
<Step>
Our web crawlers comb public web profiles and company websites on a monthly basis to maintain a list of the Benefits Producers, Account Managers, and Vendor Specialists that are currently employed at each brokerage.
</Step>
<Step>
We partner with several leading contact vendors to enrich that list with the emails / phone numbers for those individuals.
</Step>
<Step>
We independently verify the contact information using a tool called ZeroBounce.
</Step>
</Steps>
